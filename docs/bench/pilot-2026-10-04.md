# Receipt pilot, 2026-10-04

This is a calibration run. It sets the bar and checks whether the receipt task has enough headroom to stay the hero task. Its numbers are not evidence, and no headline or claim may use them (`docs/bench/prereg-nebius.md`, sections 5 and 11.6).

## Outcome

The bar is 0.40 and the headroom is 0.187, so CORD stays the hero task. Banking77 was not started.

- Ultra with a plain prompt got 74 of the 150 pilot receipts exactly right (0.493). Section 5 of the pre-registration sets the bar at that accuracy minus 0.05, rounded down to a multiple of 0.05, which gives 0.40.
- Lightning got 46 of 150 right (0.307), both with its plain prompt (A3-pilot) and with the configuration Stage 2 picked for it (A5-pilot). Headroom is 74/150 - 46/150 = 28/150 = 0.187, above the 0.10 the gate asks for.
- Ultra got right every receipt that Lightning got right, plus 28 more. The paired 95% interval for that gap is [0.123, 0.247], so even its lower end clears 0.10.

## What ran

All four arms ran on 2026-10-04 between 17:46 and 18:05 UTC, one after another, at commit `bd7e77b` on `hackathon/nebius`. The commands are the pilot commands of prereg section 5, plus `--out` and `--max-usd`:

```

architect bench run $PILOT --models lightning --max-candidates 4 --label A5-pilot --max-usd 0.99
```

`$PROFILE` is the profile copied to `examples/bench/pilot-2026-10-04/profile.json` (its sha256 matches the one in each `run.json`), and `$OUT` is a scratch directory outside the repository.

- Suite `v2`, digest `8a01acbd031c5f6f3e5918979c1bbdb89018cd948671a57d9cee23288c5e1497`, public tier, task `receipts`, rule version `receipts-v2`.
- Settings: `examples/bench/suite-v2/receipts.json` as committed and not yet frozen. It holds the placeholder bar of 0.80, a per-run budget of $0.02 and 60 seconds, invalid output counted as a miss with at most 2% allowed, an input limit of 8,192 characters, and reply parsing `first`.
- Each arm built on the rehearsal floor (100 receipts, 50 practice and 50 exam, `cases_sha256` `7c3eff3f...`) and then answered all 150 pilot receipts (`cases_sha256` `5e81a4ca...`). No build step saw a pilot receipt.

## Bar and headroom

The bar is plain Ultra's pilot accuracy minus 0.05, rounded down to a multiple of 0.05 (prereg section 5). With k = 74 receipts right, the integer form gives (floor(2k / 15) - 1) / 20 = (floor(148 / 15) - 1) / 20 = (9 - 1) / 20 = 0.40. The direct form agrees: 0.493 - 0.05 = 0.443, rounded down to 0.40.

Headroom is A1-pilot's accuracy minus the better of A3-pilot and A5-pilot. They tie at 46 right, so headroom = 74/150 - 46/150 = 28/150 = 0.187. The gate asks for at least 0.10, which is 15 receipts out of 150. It passes, and CORD stays the hero task.

Freezing the bar is the bar script's job (prereg section 5). This run left `examples/bench/suite-v2/receipts.json` unchanged.

## Other things the pilot settles or flags

These feed the bar script and the pre-registration. This run changed none of them.

- Every arm had 0 invalid runs in 150, under the 3-run line of prereg section 5, so all four keep `reply_json` `first` and none moves to `schema`.
- Prereg section 5 sets the per-run budget at twice the largest reservation an A1-pilot run made. The rows keep only the bench's sized reservation for one Ultra call, $0.007074, built from the longest receipt with each byte counted as a token and a 512-token reply. Twice that is $0.0141, under the $0.02 placeholder. Since the rows do not record each run's own reservation, the bar script has to state which figure it doubles.
- Ultra did much worse than the plan assumed. The plan and prereg section 5 simulated the bar for a true Ultra accuracy between 0.75 and 0.95, and the pilot measured 0.493. At a true rate of 0.493, Ultra would get 40 or more right on a 100-receipt exam about 98% of the time, so the bar still works as intended. The H2 power figures in prereg section 7.6 assume A1 at 85% and should be recomputed near 50%.
- Super got 75 right to Ultra's 74, at 30% of Ultra's cost per receipt. The 29 receipts where the two disagreed split 15 to 14.
- For A5, Stage 2 kept the plain configuration (retries 0, verifier depth 0), the one A3 runs. No configuration reached the placeholder bar of 0.80 on the practice receipts, and in that case Stage 2 prefers a configuration that stayed within the per-run budget, then the one with fewer components (prereg section 6). If no Lightning configuration reaches 0.40 on the evidence floor's practice receipts in the main run, A5 will again be the plain configuration, and H1a and H1b will test nearly the same pair.
- A3 and A5 ran the same configuration, but their replies were not all identical: field accuracy on the pilot receipts was 0.757 and 0.760, and practice accuracy was 0.22 and 0.24.
